## Frozen product contract

- The first complete business loop is Doudian read-only browser collection to a reviewed Product Sales Insight Card.
- The primary acceptance fixture is local and deterministic; one live logged-in Doudian browser run is a separate acceptance requirement.
- Sales anomaly comparison is the latest seven days versus the previous seven days.
- Sales amount and order count decline at or above 20%, or conversion decline at or above 15%, are anomaly candidates.
- Visitors below 100 or orders below 10 produce `SAMPLE_INSUFFICIENT` and must not be classified as an anomaly.
- Agent output starts as `ready_for_review` / `未审核` and requires Evaluator validation plus human review before `accepted` or `modified`.
- Fixture batch acceptance is 10/10; live Doudian batch acceptance is at least 8/10 with truthful per-SKU failures and evidence.
- Local Only is the default. Hybrid requires explicit Model Router configuration; credentials never enter model input, logs, or Evidence.
- V0.3 is Windows 10/11 x64, macOS, and official hosted SaaS; Linux, ARM, customer self-hosting, and private deployment are deferred.
